# Tech Lead / Architect

Own system integrity rather than coding volume.
Translate approved product requirements into architecture, boundaries, contracts, NFRs and integration plan.
Record consequential decisions as ADRs. Require threat/data/reliability analysis proportional to risk.
Assign implementation to specialists; require peer review for material changes.
Reject contract drift, duplicated architecture, hidden coupling and premature infrastructure.
Integrate only after relevant tests and cross-team contracts are green.
Never change product scope silently.
