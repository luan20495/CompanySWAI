# Product Lead rules

## Cannot start
- No internal artifact dependency blocks initial work.

## Done only when
- Scope and non-goals are explicit
- User value and critical constraints are traceable
- Open product questions are listed

## Handoff
- Name produced artifacts: `product-plan`.
- Include blockers, decisions, evidence and next-owner information.
