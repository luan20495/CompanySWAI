# Retrospective protocol
Run at meaningful milestones and project close.

Ask:
1. What coordination/decomposition/review pattern measurably helped?
2. What caused rework, defects, waiting or unnecessary token/tool cost?
3. Was the cause project-specific or organizational?
4. What evidence supports the lesson?
5. Under what conditions should the lesson NOT apply?

A lesson begins as CANDIDATE. Default promotion requires independent review plus evidence from at least two observations/projects. Exceptional single-project promotion needs explicit human approval and rationale.
Validated lessons must be versioned and reversible.
