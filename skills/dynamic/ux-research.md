# UX research

Separate what users do from what they say. State the research question, method (interviews, usability tests, analytics, surveys), sample and its limits before drawing conclusions. Capture user needs as jobs/journeys with the evidence behind each; label findings as observed fact, interpretation or hypothesis, and mark untested assumptions for validation. Prioritise by frequency and severity; avoid designing for personas not backed by evidence. Deliver research as traceable insights linked to requirements, with open questions and recommended validation steps.
